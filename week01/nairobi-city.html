My Favourite City

My favourite city is Nairobi. Nairobi is the capital city of Kenya and the biggest city in the country. It is a busy place where many people live and work. Nairobi is special because it has a national park right next to the city, where you can see lions, giraffes and zebras. The weather is usually cool and pleasant, and the city has many trees and green spaces. People from many tribes and countries live there, so you will hear different languages and enjoy different kinds of food. Many people also travel to Nairobi to look for jobs, go to school or do business.
